Help for DePrint.

DePrint keeps separate browser profiles, each with its own proxy, fingerprint, cookies and logins, so the sites you use see a different browser in each one. It runs on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Get DePrint

  • Mac (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): download the latest DMG from GitHub.
  • iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later): get DePrint from the App Store.

Free trial and license

Both apps are free for 3 days, then $4.99 once. The Mac and the iPhone/iPad apps are bought separately.

  • Mac: buy a license, then paste the key from your purchase email in Settings › License. To move it to another Mac, choose Remove from this Mac first.
  • iPhone and iPad: tap Start free trial when you first open a profile. To keep DePrint, tap Unlock. On a new device, tap Restore purchases.

When something isn't working

  • A proxy check fails: check the proxy's address, port, user name and password, and that the proxy works from your network. A profile won't open while its proxy can't be reached, so it never falls back to your own connection.
  • A site still links your profiles: give each profile its own proxy, and don't sign in to the same account in two profiles.

Still stuck? Email linyoutian.loyot@gmail.com with your DePrint version, your macOS or iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. On the Mac, Help › Copy Diagnostics puts a report on the clipboard that you can read before sharing. Leave passwords, 2FA keys and proxy passwords out of your message.

Privacy

DePrint has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and your profiles stay on your device. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
