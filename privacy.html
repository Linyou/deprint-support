Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026. This policy covers DePrint for Mac and DePrint for iPhone and iPad, made by Youtian Lin.

DePrint has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. There is no DePrint server that receives your profiles, your browsing or your passwords.

What stays on your device

Your profiles stay on the device you made them on: their settings, proxies, fingerprints, cookies and website data, and the website passwords and 2FA keys you give them. DePrint doesn't sync them anywhere.

  • Mac: everything is in DePrint's data folder (~/Library/Application Support/DePrint). Passwords, proxy passwords and 2FA keys are encrypted there with a key kept in your Mac's login Keychain.
  • iPhone and iPad: passwords, proxy passwords and 2FA keys are kept in the Keychain on that device only. Each profile's website data is kept by iOS in a separate store for that profile. Your device's own backups may include the profile list and website data, as they do for other apps.

What DePrint connects to

  • The websites you open in a profile, through that profile's proxy when it has one. They see what any website sees, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • IP lookup services, to show a profile's exit IP, location and time zone: ip-api.com, ipwho.is, ipinfo.io, get.geojs.io and cloudflare.com on the Mac; ipwho.is, ipinfo.io and api.ip.sb on iPhone and iPad. These requests go through the profile's proxy, so they see the proxy's address. Without a proxy they see yours.
  • bash.ws, only when you run the DNS leak test on a Mac profile's start page.
  • db-ip.com: DePrint for Mac downloads the free DB-IP location database from there to look up locations offline.
  • Warm-up and automation (Mac) visit the sites you set up, or a built-in list of news, weather and shopping sites, through the profile's proxy.
  • Updates (Mac): once a day DePrint fetches a small signed file from GitHub (github.com/Linyou/deprint-releases) to see if a new version is out. GitHub sees your IP address, as it does for any download.
  • License (Mac): when you enter a license key, and once a day after that, DePrint sends the key and a name for this Mac ("DePrint on" followed by your Mac's name) to Lemon Squeezy's license service to check it.

Purchases

  • Mac: DePrint is sold through Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy handles your payment and your email address under its own privacy policy. We see the order and the email address you buy with.
  • iPhone and iPad: purchases go through Apple. DePrint only learns from the App Store whether you started the free trial and whether DePrint is unlocked. To time the trial, DePrint keeps the latest time it has seen in the Keychain on your device.

What we receive

Nothing, unless you write to us. If you email support, we receive your message and what you attach. DePrint for Mac's Help › Copy Diagnostics puts the app and system versions and the last lines of DePrint's log on the clipboard, so you can read them before sharing. Leave passwords, 2FA keys and proxy passwords out of support messages.
